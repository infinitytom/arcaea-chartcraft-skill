# Revision case: Your Reality (Kenji Mizuno Remix), 2026-10-23

Evidence scope: one owner's feedback across three editable drafts, actual local
audio analysis and read-only official AFF inspections. This is a reusable failure
analysis, not an official quality standard or a successful chart template.
No song audio, artwork, official chart or identifiable official event sequence
is distributed with this reference.

## What failed

The first draft restricted blue/red snakes to their usual halves and placed the
other hand's floor notes in its own half. Small S-like paths appeared at fixed
bar intervals. An automated natural-hand conflict count of zero hid the absence
of actual cross-field choreography. The user rejected the lack of tension and
出张 and explicitly requested network cover art instead of an original placeholder.

The second draft enlarged trajectories and assigned free-hand cross-field floor
notes, but kept much of the first draft's timing and short-chain schedule. The
user still found it too simple for its FTR 10 label, mechanical, unoriginal and
unattractive. More reach events and larger spans had not changed the phrase
organization. A rating label and valid package were inadequate evidence.

## What changed in the more acceptable draft

The third draft rebuilt sampling and object organization from actual audio.
Demucs separated four candidate layers; Basic Pitch ONNX produced vocal/other
pitch candidates. The author selected phrase roles rather than taking stem RMS
as musical priority. A proposed burst was changed to a release after analysis
showed percussion and other-layer activity falling away.

The arrangement used continuous moving sky phrases, floor/sky compound accents,
longer single-Arc sentences, paired motion at separated heights and a phrase-scale
rotation. Music anchors were re-expressed across sections instead of assigning
short snakes every fixed number of bars. Network artwork came from the official
SEGA announcement for the actual release, with provenance and local-use status.

Hand-route review found accidental approximately 135 ms high-sky-to-diagonal-floor
jumps while the other hand was occupied. The planner was corrected to remember
sustain endpoints/heads and preserve local contact-layer continuity when needed.
Prepared sweeps and cross-field reaches remained. Remaining prompts were recorded,
not hidden to obtain a zero-warning claim.

The owner's final response was “这张还可以” (“this chart is okay”) and a request
to update the skill on GitHub. That supports keeping the direction as this
owner's preference; it does not establish a universal FTR 10 recipe. No separate
device-playtest, listening-review or engine-render result was established by the
assistant's automated checks. Gesture totals, chain lengths and zero exact-repeat
diagnostics were not treated as proof of beauty, novelty or a calibrated rating.

## What future revisions should preserve

Treat musical selection, phrase organization and whole-hand routes as separate
design tasks. Fix the source of feedback before tuning density/motion flags.
Read actual official configurations when using them as evidence, and state whether
the evidence was AFF geometry, aggregate statistics, a viewed render or actual
playtesting. Preserve user-approved anchors only when the user has approved them;
accepting one overall draft does not retroactively validate every prior timing.
